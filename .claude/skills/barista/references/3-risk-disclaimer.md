# § 3 · Risk Disclaimer

## Health & Safety Warnings

### Caffeine Sensitivity
- Espresso contains ~63mg caffeine per shot; multiple drinks can exceed safe daily limits
- Pregnant or nursing individuals should consult healthcare providers
- Individuals with anxiety, heart conditions, or sleep disorders should monitor intake
- Recommendation: Stop caffeine intake after 2pm to avoid sleep disruption

### Allergen Warnings
- Milk and milk alternatives carry allergen risks:
  - **Dairy milk:** Lactose and casein allergies
  - **Oat milk:** May contain gluten or cross-contamination (check supplier)
  - **Soy milk:** Soy allergen; thyroid implications with excessive consumption
  - **Nut milks:** Tree nut allergens (almond, hazelnut, macadamia)
- Always ask customers about allergies before preparing drinks
- Cross-contamination risk: use separate steam wands or clean thoroughly between drinks

### Burns & Hot Liquid Hazards
- Espresso machines operate at 90-96°C
- Steamed milk reaches 60-65°C (can cause burns)
- Always warn customers before handing hot drinks
- Use appropriate cup sleeves for insulation
- Never leave machines unattended

### Respiratory & Ergonomic
- Prolonged steam inhalation can affect respiratory health
- Repetitive tamping and pouring can cause wrist/arm strain
- Ensure proper ventilation in café environments
- Use ergonomic techniques to prevent injury

## Equipment Warnings

- **Espresso machines:** High pressure (9+ bar) requires proper training
- **Grinders:** Burrs rotate at high speed; keep hands clear
- **Steam wands:** Pressurized steam can cause severe burns
- **Hot water lines:** Constant high temperature poses scalding risk

## Food Safety

- Milk left at room temperature spoils within 2 hours
- Clean steam wands immediately after use to prevent bacterial growth
- Never reuse cooled milk
- Maintain equipment at proper hygiene standards (see § 10 Anti-Patterns #5)

## Regulatory Compliance

This skill assumes operation within food safety and health regulations of your jurisdiction. Always:
- Follow local food handling certifications
- Maintain required hygiene permits
- Comply with caffeine labeling requirements
- Ensure allergen information is available to customers
