# § 7 · Standards & Reference

## Coffee Quality Standards

### SCA (Specialty Coffee Association) Standards

**Coffee Grading Scale**
- 90-100: Outstanding
- 85-89.99: Very Good
- 80-84.99: Good
- Below 80: Below Specialty

**Espresso Shot Standards**
- Dose: 18-20g (double), 7-9g (single)
- Yield: 1:2 ratio (18g in → 36g out)
- Time: 25-30 seconds total extraction
- Temperature: 92-96°C

### Q-Grader Certification

Q-Graders hold professional coffee quality assessment certification. Certification covers:
- Cupping protocols and scoring
- Coffee chemistry and processing
- Sensory evaluation
- Defect identification

### SCAE (now SCA Europe) Standards

Pan-European standards for barista training and coffee quality, including:
- Espresso preparation standards
- Milk steaming specifications
- Coffee equipment calibration
- Professional conduct guidelines

## Extraction Science Reference

### Under-Extraction (Sour)
- **Indicators:** Sour, weak, thin body, watery
- **TDS (Total Dissolved Solids):** <18%
- **Causes:** Grind too coarse, dose too low, yield too low, temp too low
- **Solutions:** Grind finer, increase dose, increase yield, check temperature

### Over-Extraction (Bitter)
- **Indicators:** Bitter, harsh, burnt, dry mouth
- **TDS:** >22%
- **Causes:** Grind too fine, dose too high, yield too high, temp too high, prolonged contact
- **Solutions:** Grind coarser, decrease dose, decrease yield, slightly lower temperature

### Ideal Extraction (Balanced)
- **TDS:** 18-22%
- **Indicators:** Sweet, balanced, complex, pleasant acidity
- **Visual:** Golden-brown liquid (not black or pale)
- **Crema:** Golden with tiger-striping (brown/tan patterns)

## Milk Science Reference

### Milk Composition (% by weight)
- **Protein:** 3-3.5% (casein micelles create microfoam structure)
- **Lactose:** 4.6-4.9% (caramelizes when heated, creates sweetness)
- **Fat:** 3.5-4% (almond, light cream: varies)
- **Water:** ~87%

### Milk Steaming Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Temperature | Action | Duration |
|-------|---|---|---|
| **Aeration** | 40-50°C | Introduce air; wand 1cm below surface | 3-5 sec |
| **Blending** | 50-65°C | Roll and blend; integrate air into foam | 10-15 sec |
| **Smooth finish** | 65°C | Straighten wand; create symmetrical whirlpool | 2-3 sec |

**Result:** 10-15% air (3-5mm bubbles) + dense milk = creamy microfoam

### Milk Alternatives Performance

| Milk Type | Steaming Difficulty | Heat Stability | Latte Art | Notes |
|-----------|---|---|---|---|
| **Whole Dairy** | Easy | Excellent | Excellent | Standard baseline |
| **Oat** | Good | Good | Very Good | Brand varies; test each batch |
| **Soy** | Medium | Good | Good | Scorches easily; slightly lower temp |
| **Almond** | Difficult | Fair | Poor | Very prone to scalding |
| **Cashew** | Medium | Good | Good | Promising; newer technology |
| **Coconut** | Very Difficult | Poor | Very Poor | Separates; difficult to texture |

## Flavor Reference

### Tasting Wheel Categories

**Acidity Notes**
- Bright, lively, sharp, complex (desirable)
- Sour, bitter, astringent (extraction problems)

**Body**
- Light (tea-like)
- Medium (balanced)
- Heavy (syrupy, chocolate)

**Flavor Families**
- Fruit (citrus, berries, stone fruit)
- Floral (jasmine, rose, lavender)
- Nutty (almond, hazelnut, walnut)
- Spice (cinnamon, cardamom, pepper)
- Earthy (soil, leather, tobacco)
- Sweet (chocolate, caramel, honey)

## Espresso Drink Standards

### Traditional Italian Proportions (by volume)
- **Espresso:** 100% coffee
- **Ristretto:** 1:1 ratio (small, concentrated)
- **Lungo:** 1:3 ratio (long, more water)
- **Macchiato:** 1 part espresso, small dot microfoam
- **Cortado:** 1:1 espresso to steamed milk
- **Cappuccino:** 1 part espresso, 1 part milk, 1 part foam
- **Flat White:** 1 part espresso, 2 parts velvety milk
- **Latte:** 1 part espresso, 3-4 parts steamed milk, thin foam layer

## Safety Standards

### Temperature Safety Thresholds
- Espresso machine: 90-96°C (safe working range)
- Steamed milk: 60-65°C (prevents mouth burn; preserves sweetness)
- Beyond 70°C: Potential for burns; milk flavor degradation

### Hygiene Standards
- Steam wand cleaned immediately after use (no buildup)
- Group head backflushed daily
- Portafilter basket replaced monthly or as needed
- All surfaces food-safe and sanitized
- Milk stored at proper temperature (kept cold until steaming)
